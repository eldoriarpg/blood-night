# The ready-made mobs

These mobs come with BloodNight in its [mob store](store.md). Install the ones you want. You can change them,
copy them or delete them.

Their names show in each player's language, English or German so far. Players without the
[resource pack](../nights/atmosphere.md#the-resource-pack) may see the English names.

The first 33 have double health, only spawn during blood nights and go away when the night ends. The Blood Dragon is
different, see below.

## Zombies

Husks, drowned, zombie villagers and zombified piglins can become these too, when no mob of their own fits.

| ID | What it does |
|---|---|
| `armored_zombie` | Wears full diamond armour. |
| `knight_zombie` | Wears chainmail and a golden axe. Sometimes knocks you up and slows you down. Sometimes blocks your hit with a shield sound. |
| `invisible_zombie` | Invisible. When you hit it, it shows up for 10 seconds. |
| `speed_zombie` | Very fast. |

## Skeletons

| ID | What it does |
|---|---|
| `invisible_skeleton` | Invisible. |
| `magic_skeleton` | Its arrows give you a random bad effect: slowness, harm, nausea, levitation, weakness or poison. |

## Creepers

| ID | What it does |
|---|---|
| `speed_creeper` | Fast, with a short fuse. Its explosion sets fire. |
| `toxic_creeper` | Leaves a strong poison cloud when it explodes. |
| `unstable_creeper` | Charged, with a huge explosion. Explodes right away when you hit it. |
| `nervous_powered_creeper` | Charged, fast and explodes almost at once. |
| `ender_creeper` | Teleports close to you when you are far away. |
| `ghost_creeper` | Rides an invisible vex, so it flies. |

## Endermen

| ID | What it does |
|---|---|
| `fearful_enderman` | Comes for the nearest player. Its hits blind you. |
| `toxic_enderman` | Leaves a poison cloud where it teleports from. |

## Phantoms

| ID | What it does |
|---|---|
| `fearful_phantom` | Glows. Its hits slow you down. |
| `fire_phantom` | Carries a blaze. |
| `phantom_soul` | Glows but is invisible. Its hits blind you. |

## Witches

| ID | What it does |
|---|---|
| `fire_wizard` | Throws a large fireball every 5 seconds. |
| `thunder_wizard` | Marks where you stand. A moment later, lightning strikes there. |
| `wither_wizard` | Throws a wither skull every 5 seconds. |

## Spider riders

Cave spiders can become these too.

| ID | What it does |
|---|---|
| `blaze_rider` | A spider with a blaze on its back. |
| `speed_skeleton_rider` | A fast spider with a skeleton on its back. |
| `wither_skeleton_rider` | A fast spider with a wither skeleton on its back. |

## Slimes

| ID | What it does |
|---|---|
| `toxic_slime` | Leaves a poison cloud when it dies. |

## Newer mobs

| ID | What it does |
|---|---|
| `storm_breeze` | Fires three wind charges at you every 6 seconds. |
| `spore_bogged` | Its arrows poison you and make you dizzy. Leaves a poison cloud when it dies. |
| `volley_pillager` | Fires two extra arrows every 4 seconds while it sees you. |
| `tide_drowned` | Throws a trident every 3 seconds while you are in water. |
| `swarm_silverfish` | Fast. When you hit it, every silverfish nearby comes for you. |
| `assassin_spider` | Leaps onto you from up to 12 blocks away, even up into trees. Takes no fall damage. |

## The End

| ID | What it does |
|---|---|
| `launcher_enderman` | Grabs you. Sneak within a second, or it carries you up to 20 blocks into the air and lets go. |
| `grabber_enderman` | Every 10 seconds it grabs mobs around it and puts them down in front of you. |
| `fuse_enderman` | Lays lit TNT next to you every 6 seconds. It hurts but breaks no blocks. |
| `blood_dragon` | The ender dragon of a [dragon fight](../nights/nether-and-end.md#the-dragon-fight). Twice the health, harder hits, dragon fireballs and lightning, its hits wither. It never spawns by chance. |

Open any of them in the [web editor](../editor/sessions.md) to see how they work. They are good examples for your
own mobs.
