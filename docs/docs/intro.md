# Welcome to BloodNight

BloodNight makes some nights special. We call them **blood nights**.

On a blood night, the sky gets dark and red. Some of the monsters that spawn turn into **special mobs**. A special
mob can be faster, stronger or sneakier than a normal one. It can throw fireballs, call lightning or turn invisible.

You decide how your special mobs behave. You build their behaviour in a **web editor** by connecting little boxes
called nodes. No coding needed. Your server turns those boxes into fast Java code.

BloodNight comes with ready-made special mobs, so it works right after you install it.

## What you can do

- Choose which nights become blood nights: by chance, by the moon, every few nights, and more.
- Show a boss bar, play spooky sounds and send messages when a blood night begins.
- Make monsters hit harder and block some commands during a blood night.
- Build your own special mobs and give them equipment, riders and drops.
- Edit everything together with your team in the browser.
